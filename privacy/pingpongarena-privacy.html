Privacy Policy for Ping Pong Arena

Ping Pong Arena is a game application developed by jgmicro. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service. This policy applies to both the Android and iOS versions of the app.

1. Data Collection and Use

Firebase Realtime Database: To provide the "World Ranking" feature, this app collects the nickname or name you provide. This information is stored on Google Firebase servers and is used solely to display rankings to other users. No other sensitive personal information is collected during this process.

Firebase Analytics: This app uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as which game modes are played and how long a game lasts. This data is used only to improve the game and does not identify you personally.

No Personal Data Collection: Other than the ranking nickname, we do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as email addresses or phone numbers.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

This app displays advertisements via Google AdMob. These third-party ad networks may collect non-personal device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID: AAID on Android or IDFA on iOS) and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, as governed by their respective privacy policies. On iOS, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking when asked.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchases in this app are processed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). The developer never collects or stores your card or payment information.

4. Data Security and Sharing

The developer (jgmicro) does not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Ranking data is only visible within the app for competitive play purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

This game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.
