Privacy Policy for Fortune Mate

Fortune Mate is a fortune-telling and tarot application developed by jgmicro. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. Data Collection and Use

Local Device Storage Only: To provide fortune, compatibility, and tarot readings, this app asks you to enter names and birth dates (for yourself and, optionally, other people such as family or friends). This information is stored only locally on your device (using SharedPreferences on Android and UserDefaults on iOS) and is never transmitted to, or stored on, any external server operated by us.

Optional "Leave a Question" Feature: If you choose to send a question to the developer through the app, the text you type is sent to us by email together with your device identifier, country, and language. This is sent only when you press the send button.

No Automatic Personal Data Collection: We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or other directly identifying information. The app collects anonymous usage statistics as described in Section 2.

2. Third-Party Services

Advertising (Google AdMob): This app displays advertisements, including banner ads and rewarded video ads, via Google AdMob. These third-party ad networks may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID on Android or the IDFA on iOS) and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, as governed by their respective privacy policies. You can find more information about Google's data practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Consent and Tracking Choices: Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and certain US states), the app shows a consent or privacy-choices message provided by Google. On iOS, the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking. You can change these choices at any time in the app or your device settings.

Analytics (Firebase Analytics): This app uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage events, such as which menu or question category you select, along with app and device identifiers. This helps us understand which features are used. More information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

In-App Purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store): This app offers optional in-app purchases (a full-access pass and a banner-removal option) processed entirely through Google Play (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS). We do not collect, see, or store any payment card information; all payment processing is handled by Google or Apple in accordance with their own privacy and security policies.

3. Data Security and Sharing

The developer (jgmicro) does not sell, share, or transmit your entered names or birth dates to any third party. All such data remains stored locally on your own device and can be deleted at any time by removing the entry within the app or uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. All data entered in this app is stored locally on the user's own device. The only exception is a question that the user voluntarily chooses to send. Local data can be removed at any time by deleting the entry in the app or uninstalling the app.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
