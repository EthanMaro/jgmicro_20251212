Privacy Policy for Fortune Mate

Fortune Mate is a fortune-telling and tarot application developed by jgmicro. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

1. Data Collection and Use

Local Device Storage Only: To provide fortune, compatibility, and tarot readings, this app asks you to enter names and birth dates (for yourself and, optionally, other people such as family or friends). This information is stored only locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences and is never transmitted to, or stored on, any external server operated by us.

No Automatic Personal Data Collection: We do not automatically collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as email addresses or phone numbers.

2. Third-Party Services

Advertising (Google AdMob): This app displays advertisements, including banner ads and rewarded video ads, via Google AdMob. These third-party ad networks may collect non-personal device identifiers (such as AAID) and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, as governed by their respective privacy policies. You can find more information about Google's data practices at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing): This app offers optional in-app purchases (a full-access pass and a banner-removal option) processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. We do not collect, see, or store any payment card information; all payment processing is handled by Google Play in accordance with its own privacy and security policies.

3. Data Security and Sharing

The developer (jgmicro) does not sell, share, or transmit your entered names or birth dates to any third party. All such data remains stored locally on your own device and can be deleted at any time by removing the entry within the app or uninstalling the app.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. All data entered in this app is stored locally on the user's own device and is never transmitted to us, so it can be removed at any time simply by deleting the entry in the app or uninstalling the app.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
