Privacy Policy for IP View

This app shows your public IP address, local network information (including your Wi-Fi network name), and an approximate location.

The app gets your IP address and approximate location from third-party IP lookup services. The location is based on your IP address and is registered by your internet provider. It is not your exact location, and the app does not use GPS.

The Wi-Fi network name is read from your device only to show it on the screen. It is not stored or shared.

The app does not store or share this information. It is only shown on your screen.

The app shows a map using Google Maps, and it shows ads using Google AdMob. These services may collect device identifiers to show ads and maps. Please see Google's privacy policy for details.

The developer does not store, sell, or share any personal data.
