Privacy Policy for IP View

This app shows your public IP address, local network information (including your Wi-Fi network name), and your location on a map.

The app gets your IP address and approximate location from third-party IP lookup services. This location is based on your IP address and is not your exact location.

If you allow location permission, the app can also show your precise location (GPS) and your street address when you use the my-location button. To find the address, your coordinates are sent to the location service of your device's operating system (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). This is done only when you tap the button and the app is open. The developer does not receive, store, or share your precise location. You can turn this permission off at any time in your device settings.

The Wi-Fi network name is read from your device only to show it on the screen. It is not stored or shared.

The app does not store or share this information. It is only shown on your screen.

The app shows a map using Google Maps, and it shows ads using Google AdMob. These services may collect device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, and other data to show maps and ads. In some regions, such as the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time with "Ad privacy settings" on the main screen. Please see Google's privacy policy for details.

The developer does not store, sell, or share any personal data.
